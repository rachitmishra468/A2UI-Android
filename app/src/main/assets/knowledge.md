# Rango AI Assistant - Knowledge Base

## 🍽️ About the Restaurant
- **Name:** Luxe Dining (Rango's Home)
- **Location:** 123 Gourmet Street, Food City.
- **Operating Hours:** 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM (Every day).
- **Ambience:** Fine dining with a modern touch.

## 🚚 Delivery & Shipping
- **Delivery Time:** Usually 30-45 minutes depending on traffic.
- **Minimum Order:** ₹200 for free delivery. Below ₹200, a flat fee of ₹40 applies.
- **Coverage:** We deliver within a 10km radius of the restaurant.

## 💳 Payments & Refunds
- **Accepted Payments:** Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.
- **Cancellation Policy:** Orders can be cancelled within 2 minutes of placing. Once the status changes to "Preparing", cancellation is not possible.
- **Refunds:** Refunds for cancelled orders are processed within 5-7 business days.

## 🥗 Dietary Information
- **Vegetarian (Veg):** Marked with a green dot. Prepared in a separate section of the kitchen.
- **Non-Vegetarian (Non-Veg):** Prepared with the highest quality standards.
- **Allergies:** If you have nut or gluten allergies, please mention it in the order notes or call us directly.

## 📅 Table Bookings
- **Advance Booking:** Recommended for weekends.
- **Holding Time:** We hold your table for a maximum of 15 minutes past your booking time.
- **Group Bookings:** For groups larger than 10, please call our manager at +1 234-567-890.

## 🎁 Loyalty & Rewards
- **Rango Points:** Earn 5 points for every ₹100 spent.
- **Redemption:** 100 points = ₹50 discount on your next order.
